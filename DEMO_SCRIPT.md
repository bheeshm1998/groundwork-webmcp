# SweetSpot demo script (about 90 seconds)

## 0:00–0:10 — The problem

**On screen:** SweetSpot landing page, then click **Start planning in San Francisco**.

**Say:**

“Choosing where to live is not one search. It is a negotiation between work, daily essentials, and the places that make life enjoyable. SweetSpot turns all of those needs into one map.”

## 0:10–0:25 — Show the agent, not a chatbot

**On screen:** Keep SweetSpot and ChatGPT visible together. Paste the first starter prompt.

**Say:**

“This is not a chatbot pasted onto a map. Through WebMCP, ChatGPT can read and operate the exact workspace I see. I’ll ask it for an 8-minute bike ride to City Hall, a 5-minute walk to a supermarket, and a 4-minute walk to a park.”

**Prompt:**

“Help me find a genuinely focused area in San Francisco: within an 8-minute bike ride of San Francisco City Hall, a 5-minute walk of a supermarket, and a 4-minute walk of parks. Build the complete plan, show three geographically distinct options, and explain each trade-off.”

## 0:25–0:42 — Let the product prove the work

**On screen:** Show the live “Agent · doing…” ticker, named per-layer progress, map layers, and results appearing.

**Say:**

“The calculation happens privately in this browser on a real directed street network. The interface stays usable and shows exactly which layer is running. In one agent action, SweetSpot builds the full plan and ranks three distinct neighborhoods.”

## 0:42–0:56 — Make the result concrete

**On screen:** Point to the 9.05 km² result and the Hayes Valley, Nob Hill, and South of Market cards. Open one detail panel.

**Say:**

“Instead of saying that most of the city works, this prompt finds about nine square kilometres. The options are genuinely separated, every time is visible, and SweetSpot explains the weakest margin instead of hiding it behind one score.”

## 0:56–1:14 — The human handoff moment

**On screen:** Ask ChatGPT to request a personal boundary. Draw a polygon on the map and double-click to finish. Let the result update.

**Say:**

“But an agent should know when human judgment matters. I can say, ‘Only consider this part of the city.’ ChatGPT pauses, hands me the pen, and waits. I draw the area I would actually consider, and the agent continues from my decision.”

## 1:14–1:25 — Trust and control

**On screen:** Open **Workspace**. Show the You/Agent activity labels, then Undo and Share plan.

**Say:**

“Every agent and human change is attributed, visible, undoable, and shareable. Both interfaces use the same validated command path, so the map never tells a different story from the assistant.”

## 1:25–1:32 — Close

**On screen:** Return to the three results on the map.

**Say:**

“SweetSpot makes spatial planning collaborative: the agent handles the graph, the human supplies judgment, and together they reach a decision you can inspect. That is what a native agentic web experience should feel like.”

## Recording notes

- Record at 1440×900 or 1920×1080 with the browser zoom at 100%.
- Use San Francisco for the main demo because the measured starter result is tight and visually clear.
- Start from a fresh workspace and warm the page once before recording so map tiles are cached.
- Keep the cursor still while calculations run; let the live ticker and map carry the moment.
- Do not spend time reading every metric. Name the three neighborhoods, open one trade-off, then move to the drawing handoff.
- If the video limit is 60 seconds, remove the landing-page introduction and begin at the workspace.
